# Measurements: solid-angle disk and tube lights

## 1.3 Hot path, by instruction count

`RAYON_NUM_THREADS=1 valgrind --tool=callgrind --cache-sim=no --branch-sim=no`,
`crust render -s 2`, release builds (fat LTO), the pre-change binary against each
build of this change. `render_pixel` is inlined into the integrator and has no symbol
of its own; `<AreaLight as Light>::sample_li` and `Bvh::hit` do. The comparison is the
exclusive count of every function, diffed (`cgdiff.py`, scratchpad). Differences of a
few hundred thousand in USD prim ordering, rayon work stealing, the allocator and the
EXR writer move either way between runs and are not the render.

`veach_mis` has four round sphere lights and neither a disk nor a tube; `cornellbox`
has one rect light.

| build | `veach_mis` total | `sample_li` | `pdf_at_point` | `memcpy` |
|-------|-------------------|-------------|----------------|----------|
| pre-change | 7,780,258,931 | 365,172,299 | 3,563,506 | 39,823,538 |
| 1: strategies inlined | +0.10% | **+2.1%** | +17% | −0.4% |
| 2: bodies out of line, state borrowed (`ref`) | +1.2% | **+14%** | +44% | **+110%** |
| 3: small state passed by value, `Option<(Vec3A, _)>` returned | +0.11% | **+2.3%** | +10% | −0.4% |
| 4 (as built): a point and an `Option<PdfSolidAngle>` returned | **−0.03%** | **−0.76%** | +10% | −0.3% |

`Bvh::hit` is 920,524,596 on `cornellbox` in every build, and unchanged on `veach_mis`;
`cornellbox`'s render functions are unchanged in every build (its one light is a rect,
whose dispatch the change does not touch).

What each step found, by an instruction-kind histogram of `sample_li` under
`--dump-instr=yes`:
- **1:** the disk and tube bodies, inlined beside the sphere's and rect's, changed
  register allocation on the sphere path.
- **2:** borrowing the strategy's fields (`ref ellipse`, `ref view`) took the address
  of the sampler, which then lived in memory: with a 200-byte `SphericalEllipse` in the
  enum, every sphere sample copied it.
- **3:** per call exactly +2 `movaps`, +2 `mov`, +2 `movss`, +1 `test`/`je`: the
  `Option<(Vec3A, PdfSolidAngle)>` the out-of-line functions returned, 32 bytes and so
  returned in memory, merged every arm's answer in a stack slot.
- **4:** the out-of-line functions return a `Vec3A` and an `Option<PdfSolidAngle>`
  (8 bytes), both in registers, and the sampler holds only `Ω_r(π/2)` for a disk
  (`round_strategies_do_not_grow_the_sampler` pins the size). `sample_li` is now below
  the pre-change count; `pdf_at_point` is +0.36M, 0.005% of the run.

## 2.4 Newton and the two radial maps

- Newton in `ψ = φ_t`: at most 6 iterations, 2.70 on average, over a 40 × 40
  `(height, offset)` grid × 512 `u` (729,600 samples), every ellipse of at least
  1e-4 sr. In `φ` (the paper's variable) the same grid took up to 10: where `Ω_r` is
  flat (`φ → π/2` on a small eccentric ellipse) Newton stalled at `Ω_r`'s rounding.

The sweep frames take ~40 ms, so their timings are coarse; `usdlux`'s are the ones to
price a sample by. The disk rows are with the band's floor at the rect's 1e-4 sr.

**Bias check on the small disk.** The ellipse's relMSE on disk r0.1 diffuse was above
area's, which could have been a bias of ~0.2%. At 16, 64 and 256 spp against the
reference it fell 7.5e-6 → 1.1e-6 → 2.4e-7 (area: 2.3e-6 → 1.4e-7 → 3.1e-8): no floor,
so noise — the area map stratifies a small disk better under `openqmc`.

**Profile of the ellipse** (`disk_r1_diffuse`, 4 spp, one thread): 652M instructions
against 290M with area sampling; `EllipseMap::omega_r` (Carlson's `R_F` + `R_J`,
inlined) is 244M of them, 37% of the run: about four `Π` per sample (the strategy's
`Ω`, and 2.7 Newton steps), ~5,000 instructions per sample in all.

**`--light-samples` (Open Question 3).** `tube_l1_r0.3_diffuse`, 4 spp ×
`--light-samples 4`, four frames: area 1.98e-3, equiangular 1.46e-4 relMSE (13.6×),
against 17.5× at 16 spp × 1. `openqmc` stratifies the arc across the draws.

## 5.3 Decisions

- **`CRUST_TUBE_SAMPLING` defaults to `equiangular`.** It has the lower relMSE than
  `area` on all 12 sweep tubes and `usdlux`, and wins at equal time on every one
  (1.32–20×; `usdlux` 1.82×). `arc` is ahead of it at equal time on three glossy cases
  only, where both win.
- **`CRUST_DISK_SAMPLING` stays `area`.** The ellipse loses at equal time on 6 of 8
  sweep disks and on `usdlux`, and on two glossy disks in relMSE alone, so no faster
  inversion would make it the default. D5's circumscribed-square alternative was not
  built: it is uniform in solid angle too, plus ~21% zero samples, so it cannot win the
  glossy cases either.
- **The disk's band floor moves from 1e-4 to 0.1 sr** (`MIN_ELLIPSE_SR`): at ~0.03 sr
  (radius 0.1 at height 1) the ellipse lost in relMSE as well (1.8×), at ~0.26 sr
  (radius 0.3) it had a third of area's relMSE.
- **Open Question 2: no band for the tube.** The back-facing half it skips does not
  shrink with distance; the smallest tube still won 7.4×.
- **Open Question 3: nothing to design.** See above.

### Equal time on ALab

ALab is the only scene with a tube or a disk, so it is the only one measured here.
**Its frame is its animation, so every figure below is one seed** (`-f 1004`). `-f`
also sets the sampler's seed, and no flag reseeds frame 1004.

Setup:
- **Stage.** Every render goes through a wrapper `.usda` that sublayers `entry.usda`,
  repeats its time metadata and units, and authors `/Render/settings` with only
  `crust:minSamplesPerPixel = 1024`. ALab authors no RenderSettings prim. With the
  wrapper no pixel ever stops early, so every render takes exactly its `-s`.
- **Reference.** 1024 spp, with both switches at `area` and `--indirect-clamp 0`.
- **Variants.**
  - Tubes: `CRUST_TUBE_SAMPLING` = `area`, `arc` or `equiangular`, with the disk at
    `area`.
  - Disk: `CRUST_DISK_SAMPLING=ellipse`, with the tube at `area`.
- **Error.** `relmse_trimmed` from `crust diff --json` against the reference.
- **Time.** `Render` min and mean over 6 interleaved reps. Each variant ran in its own
  `scripts/bench_ab.sh` pair against `area`, so the `area` row gives the range of its
  three pairs.
- **Efficiency.** `(relMSE·t_min)_area / (relMSE·t_min)_variant`, with each pair's own
  `area` time.

**The crop** is `--region 470,150,620,250` (150 × 100 px), around the oscilloscope and
the ham radios. Between `area` and `equiangular` at 64 spp it holds 87% of the
absolute difference in 6.5% of the frame. Its pixels match the full frame's, and the
crop has its own region-rendered reference.

**On the scene as authored, the switches change nothing measurable.** Across the
variants, relMSE agrees to the sixth significant figure, and every time delta (−0.7%
to +1.8% on the min) is below what timing resolves. `arc` (+1.4%) and `equiangular`
(+1.2%) cost the same on the frame, so the 0.99s are noise in `t_min`, not a loss.
There are two reasons the switches have so little to act on:
- ALab at clamp 0 is dominated by indirect fireflies.
- The round lights carry ~2.3% of the crop's direct light: a mean of 0.0032 against
  0.138.

The relMSE that the tube sampler itself can move was bounded from the 16 spp pair. The
two renders share every random draw except the tube's own (keyed sub-domains), so
their difference is the tube's NEE estimator difference alone. Over the reference,
`relsq(area − equiangular)` is at most 1.9e-5 in the crop, against 1.64. Direct
lighting alone (`crust:maxDepth = 1` in the wrapper) still left the four variants
within 0.08% of each other (trimmed 0.13809 `area`, 0.13798 `equiangular`).

**Round lights only.** To see the samplers on these lights' real geometry, a third
wrapper deactivates the other 33 lights (`active = false`), which leaves the 13
cylinders and the disk. It also renders direct light only (`crust:maxDepth = 1`).
`equiangular` wins 1.21× at equal time, `arc` 1.18× and the disk's `ellipse` 1.10×.
That is far below the sweep's 1.3–20×. The button tubes are short and thin, and they
are far from most of what they light, where any tube looks like a short segment. The
renders are ~0.11 s, so a few ms of jitter is several percent of the time.

**Bias.** The trimmed relMSE falls without a floor as the spp quadruple, where 4× per
step is expected. From 64 to 256 spp the 1024 spp reference's own variance caps the
expected drop at ~3.4×.

### Judgement against D7

- **`equiangular` stays the tube default.** It does not lose at equal time on any real
  scene. On ALab as authored it ties `area` within noise (0.99–1.00, relMSE equal to
  the sixth figure). With ALab's round lights isolated, it wins 1.21×, the most of the
  three tube strategies.
- **`area` stays the disk default.** On ALab the ellipse ties as authored (1.00) and
  wins 1.10× with the round lights isolated. But ALab's one disk is a single scene and
  not a win "on every scene with disks". The sweep and `usdlux` still show losses
  (0.30–0.67× on glossy floors and small disks), so the condition for changing the
  default is not met.

Neither conclusion changes, so the lighting design record and
`docs/light_sampling.md` §3.13 are left as they are.
